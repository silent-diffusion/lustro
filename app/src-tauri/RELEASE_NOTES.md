## What's new in Lustro 0.1.1
- **Scans happen in place.** Overview and Explore fill in live while Lustro scans; progress and Stop are in the bar under the top bar.
- **Full view** on the terrain (or press Home) takes you back to the whole drive.
- The terrain keeps slowly turning at the full view, and eases back in after you stop using it.
- **Up next** in Cleanup is its own card, with up to 8 items.
- Overview now uses the main view chosen in Settings, and long cleanup queues fit on screen.

## Privacy
Lustro works offline. The only network request is the update check, which you can turn off in Settings. Every update is signed, and Lustro checks the signature before installing.
