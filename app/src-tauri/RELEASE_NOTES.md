## What's new in Lustro 0.1.2
- **Route card** on the full terrain view: click something to see every folder on the way from the drive down to it, with sizes. Click a row to go to that folder.
- Cloud-only folders now show up as lakes on the terrain, instead of being lumped into "Other".

## Privacy
Lustro works offline. The only network request is the update check, which you can turn off in Settings. Every update is signed, and Lustro checks the signature before installing.
